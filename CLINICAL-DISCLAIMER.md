### English Version

1. **Demonstration Knowledge Base:**
   The drug-drug interaction rules, dose calculations, allergy cross-reactivity groupings, and renal/pregnancy safety criteria bundled with this project are based on openly accessible medical reference literature (e.g., WHO ATC, RxNorm, FDA, BNF, and local Jordan Food and Drug Administration (JFDA) generic listings). This data is provided solely for demonstration, testing, and architecture validation purposes.

2. **No Clinical Advice:**
   Wasfa does **not** provide clinical diagnosis, medical advice, or definitive treatment recommendations. The safety alerts and decision support indicators generated by this system must **never** replace the clinical judgement, pharmacovigilance, or independent evaluation of a licensed physician, clinical pharmacist, or qualified healthcare professional.

3. **Pluggable Architecture for Certified Providers:**
   In an actual hospital, national health system, or certified outpatient deployment, the demonstration `IDrugKnowledgeProvider` implementation **must** be substituted with an authorized, legally licensed, and continuously updated clinical drug database (such as First Databank, Wolters Kluwer Lexicomp, IBM Micromedex, or local national regulatory drug databases).
